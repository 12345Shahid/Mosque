1. Live Khutbah Translation

The Imam speaks naturally in Arabic, and the system translates the khutbah in real time. Attendees feel like they are following the khutbah directly in their own language without waiting for a recording or manual translation.

2. Multiple Languages

Users can choose languages such as English, Chinese, Bengali, Urdu, French, and more. Each person can experience the same khutbah in the language they understand best.

3. Live TV Display

The translated text can appear instantly on the mosque's TV or projector. The display is clean and easy to read while the khutbah is happening.

4. Personal Phone Translation

Attendees can scan a QR code with their phone and immediately join the live translation. No app installation is required, creating a very simple experience for visitors.

5. Real-Time Voice Translation

Instead of only reading text, attendees can listen to the translated khutbah through their headphones. This allows them to focus on the speaker rather than constantly looking at their phone.

6. Quran Ayah Detection

When the Imam recites a Quranic verse, the system recognizes it and displays the corresponding ayah and translation. This provides a more accurate experience for Quranic content.

7. Arabic + Translation View

Users can see the original Arabic alongside their selected translation. This keeps the original khutbah visible while making its meaning accessible to everyone.

8. Simple Mosque Dashboard

Mosque staff get a simple control panel where they can start a khutbah, select languages, control the display, and monitor the live session. The goal is for someone with little technical knowledge to operate it.

9. Khutbah History

Past khutbah sessions can be saved with their transcripts and translations. Mosque administrators can later review, search, or reuse previous khutbah content.

10. Live Session Management

Each khutbah works as a separate live session with its own link and QR code. Staff can start and end sessions easily, while attendees automatically receive the latest translation.

11. Custom Mosque Experience

Each mosque can have its own name, logo, branding, preferred languages, and display settings. This makes the platform feel like the mosque's own translation service rather than a generic website.

12. Analytics & Usage

Mosques can see basic information such as translation time, number of listeners, languages used, and previous sessions. This helps them understand how the service is being used.
